About

I'm a PhD student in Information Technology at the University of Parma. My research has two strands. The first is neuro-symbolic methods for predictive process monitoring, so that predictions comply with business rules and logical constraints and hold up when data is limited. The second is machine learning for predictive analytics in healthcare.

Before the PhD I received my M.S. in Computer Engineering and my B.S. in Computer, Electronic, and Telecommunications Engineering, both from the University of Parma.

Research

Neuro-symbolic predictive process monitoring

Combining neural models with logical knowledge so that predictions about running process instances are both accurate and compliant with business rules.

Neuro-symbolic AI for early sepsis diagnosis

Predicting sepsis early from clinical textual notes, using methods that encode medical knowledge alongside learned representations.
